## Patterns
1. **Profile pictures.** Winners use one of two kinds:
   - A **bold simple icon on dark** (gear, question mark, stopwatch, "So. That's How").
   - A **close-up cartoon character bust with a gesture and an expression**: desmontandoomundo (smiling guy holding a screwdriver, fiery yellow background), swoosh (winking boy making the OK sign on blue), prfpalito (cop pointing at you), manualvisual (thinking pose with a "?").

   **None of them shows a small full-body figure.** That's why our full-body Hoot reads badly in the circle. Fix: a **head-and-shoulders Hoot that fills the circle**, with a gesture (a wink plus a wing thumbs-up, or pointing his pencil at you), on a bold flat colour with a rim.
2. **A fixed cover template per page.** A small series tag at the top ("• COMO FUNCIONA · TOPIC", "HOW IT ACTUALLY WORKS"), then a big 2–3 line title with **one accent-coloured word**, one central object, and a dark background on most of them.
3. **Twist-statement titles** beat plain questions:
   - "O avião tem 180 lugares. Venderam 190."
   - "It doesn't heat your food."
   - "This traffic jam has no cause."
   - "Vendem mais passagens que assentos. De propósito."
4. **Your own body** topics hit hard: an eye twitching for no reason (1.7M); the jolt in bed as you fall asleep; why people who sleep in the day wake up wrecked (408K). That's a good sign for the sleep series.
5. **Proven topics to borrow** (seen on 2 or more pages):
   - airline overbooking (59.5K and 10.4K)
   - why you open the window shade for landing (968K)
   - traffic jams with no cause (6.2M)
   - vape vs cigarette
   - the dishwasher, microwave and washing machine "doesn't work how you think"
6. **Captions:** most pages use a short phrase at the top with a highlighted-word box, not one big centre word.
7. **Hits are spiky.** Even the big pages have many posts at 2–9K views. Consistent volume matters, and so does testing hooks.
8. **Many of these pages sell a course** ("make videos like this with AI"), so the format is getting crowded. Hoot as a recognisable character plus a recurring human (Jake) is how we stand apart.
