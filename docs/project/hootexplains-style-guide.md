# Hoot Explains: style guide (fixed from 2 Nov 2026; visual style locked 3 Nov 2026)

> Updates since then (user feedback, 3–4 Nov): Jake (a recurring human character) lives the stories and Hoot narrates. Hoot has ONE beak that opens with the voice and always faces the camera. Default length is now 30–45 s (see SCRIPT_RULES.md). The cover tag must sit below y≈300 because the Instagram grid crops covers to 3:4.

## Look
- **House style: "real"** (agreed 3 Nov 2026). This is animated 3D with a realistic finish: rounded shapes, soft lighting with reflections, NeutralToneMapping, and slightly boosted saturation. It is the default in the pipeline; never mix in other styles.
- Scenes use cross-sections and cutaways in bright daylight colours. Night scenes are allowed when the story needs them.
- Real-world objects need real-world details. Always frame the whole object, unless the shot is a deliberate close-up.

## Host
- Hoot the owl (brown, navy round glasses, teal bow tie, pencil) appears in the **first frame**.
- A small **Hoot badge** stays top-left: an orange round avatar plus a "HOOT" pill, which reads "HOOT EXPLAINS" for the first 2.6 s.
- Hoot must sit or stand somewhere that makes sense, never inside props or covered by them.

## Brand
- **Colours:** orange #ff8a3d, yellow #ffd21f (highlights), white pills, teal #21b3a0 (accents).
- **Font:** Montserrat 800/900.
- **Watermark:** @hootexplains in a small dark pill.
- **Names:** the display name is "Hoot Explains | How Things Work".

## Text on screen
- **Captions:** one big word at a time, yellow on key words.
- **Labels:** small white pills with a coloured dot, which must never overlap the badge, the watermark or faces.
- **Number chips:** metric next to US units. A "SOURCE: …" chip goes with science and health facts.
