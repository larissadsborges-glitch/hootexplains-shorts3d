## Ready replies for likely comments
- **"Gold ended in 1933, not 1971!"** Good catch. At home, the 1933–34 gold laws stopped people redeeming dollars for gold. Foreign governments could still swap dollars for gold until 15 Aug 1971. That was the last link, so the video says America "cut that link" in 1971.
- **"So it's backed by nothing?"** It's backed by law (it must be accepted for debts and taxes) and by trust. That's what "fiat money" means.
- **"Shops can refuse cash!"** True. Legal tender covers debts and taxes, not every sale. That's why the video says "pays any debt or tax".
- **"Bitcoin fixes this"** That's a fun debate. Bitcoin is also worth what people trust it's worth. Same idea, different rules 🦉
