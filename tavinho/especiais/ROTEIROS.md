# A Vida do Tavinho: 2 new episodes (scripts v1, 5 Nov 2026)

Same style as EP1: cuts about every second, face close-ups, coloured names, a red "ERRO #1" card, a meter, and a vote with costs and downsides on screen. Each one stands alone (no need to have seen EP1). Same cast plus 2 new characters: **Seu Valdir** (the boss) and **Princesa** (the mother-in-law's dog).

Rules check for both:
- the hook says who and what in the first words, and its on-screen text matches it
- the "você decide" bridge comes at about 4–5 s
- every turn is a "mas" or an "então"
- the shareable line comes before the middle
- the twist comes at about 70%
- the vote has a real cost and a downside on each side, and the episode ends on the question
- no engagement bait
- numbers are written as words
- about 155 words, so about 55 s

The scripts' JSON is in `videos/tavaudio/script.json` and `videos/tavcachorra/script.json`.
