How the store would run

Platform

Shopify for the shop, checkout and payments, plus Printful (or Printify) to print and ship each order. When someone buys, Printful prints the item with their name and ships it from a US facility. You never hold stock.

Monthly costs

  • Shopify Basic: about $29–39 a month (≈ €27–36), plus card fees per sale.
  • Printful: free plan. You pay only the base cost when an order comes in.
  • Domain (e.g. shop.hootexplains.com): about $15 a year.

Launch steps

  1. You approve the products and prices on this page.
  2. I render print-ready files (4500 × 5400 px) for each design.
  3. You open Shopify and Printful accounts and connect them.
  4. Products, copy and photos go in; a test order checks print quality.
  5. The link goes in the bio and a "Hoot merch" Short announces it.
