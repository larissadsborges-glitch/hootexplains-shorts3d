## Ready replies for likely comments
- **"Gold ended in 1933, not 1971!"** Good catch. At home, the 1933–34 gold laws stopped the Treasury and banks redeeming dollars for gold (Federal Reserve History, Gold Reserve Act). Foreign governments could still swap dollars for gold until 15 Aug 1971. That was the last link.
- **"So it's backed by nothing?"** By law (it must be accepted for debts and taxes) and by trust. That's what the Fed's own teaching material calls fiat money.
