## Ref #1 — @desmontandoomundo, "Telhas, inclinação e madeira" (roof)
- **Numbers:** ~15.4M views, 1.8M likes (11.7%), 14.5K comments, 41K reposts, **225K sends (1.5%)**, **181K saves (1.2%)**.
- **Account:** 614K followers in about 2 weeks with 16 posts. This is their pinned #1 video.
- **Format:** 2:45 long (165s), Portuguese, low-poly 3D, 720×1280.

### What it does
1. **Everyday subject.** It's about the roof of the viewer's own house, not an exotic machine. The question is "why is MY house built like this?"
2. **A chain of problems and fixes.** Each part of the roof exists to solve the previous problem:
   1. A flat concrete slab lets rain pool, and it leaks (a "goteira" crack drips).
   2. Tilting it fixes that, and tiles go on top.
   3. Water still gets under the tiles, so they get a 30% slope.
   4. The roof weighs 3 tons, so it needs a thick wood frame.
   5. Without the frame, the walls deform.
   6. Then come the ridge cap, the gutter ("calha"), the flashing ("rufo"), sideways rain, heat (+50 °C), the "air cushion" under the roof, and wind with a wire to tie it down.

   Every beat is a "but then…" moment. There's no separate re-hook timer because the whole video is re-hooks.
3. **A host character, the brand mascot.** A blocky guy in a yellow shirt, the same character as the profile picture. He opens the video waving and talking to the viewer ("Você…"). Later he stands under an umbrella in the rain, gets rained on inside the house, and sits sweating on the sofa. He gives the video scale and emotion and makes the channel recognisable.
4. **It builds in front of you.** The house assembles on screen within the first 2s: furniture, then walls, then the slab.
5. **Almost no hard cuts.** There are only 7 cuts in 165s; everything else is continuous camera glides. It feels like one long tour. There are lots of macro close-ups: water beads on tiles, wood beams, the gutter.
6. **Small labels.** Little white pill tags with a dot ("• LAJE DE CONCRETO", "• GOTEIRA", "• ÁGUA ACUMULA") sit on the object. They use small yellow text only for key terms (CALHA, RUFO, CUMEEIRA). Numbers appear as small chips ("3 TONELADAS" with red force arrows, "30%" with a ruler, "+50 °C").
7. **Captions.** One white word at a time, low in the middle of the frame, medium size. This is close to ours.
8. **Calm visuals.** No red flashes, no REWIND or SLOW MOTION chips, no shake. The physics and weather tell the story: rain, puddles that grow, drips, heat glow, red arrows for weight.
9. **Audio.** The voice is continuous with almost no pauses; I measured only one silence over 0.25s in 2:45. It's very compressed (LRA 2 LU) at -14 LUFS.
10. **CTA.** The final shot is the finished house with the host and "SABIA? COMENTE" ("Did you know? Comment").

### Why the shares and saves are so high (my read)
- It's useful to someone you know who is building or fixing a house, so people send it.
- It's a full, practical explanation, so people save it.

### What we copy
- [ ] **Mascot host:** a recurring @explainland character. Must be original, and it doubles as the profile picture.
- [ ] **Everyday topics:** "why is your X built like this" (your roof, your window, your toilet, your road, your fridge).
- [ ] **Problem → fix chain:** each beat introduces the next part as the answer to a problem shown visually.
- [ ] **Build the thing on screen in the first 2s.**
- [ ] **Fewer cuts:** long continuous camera glides between close-ups.
- [ ] **Smaller labels:** pill tags on the object, plus small number chips with arrows.
- [ ] **Drop the effects:** fewer flash and chip overlays; let weather and physics do it.
- [ ] **Tighter voice:** shorter gaps between beats.
- [ ] **Longer videos:** 2:00–2:45 works when every beat is a new problem.
- [ ] **CTA:** a short "Did you know? Comment" on the final wide shot.

## Ref #2: @explicadorapido, "¿Renting o comprar nuevo?" (Spanish)
- **Numbers:** 809K views, 11.3K likes (1.4%), 505 comments, 223 reposts, **11.6K sends (1.43%, more sends than likes!)**, 6.1K saves (0.75%).
- **Account:** 20.3K followers, 50 posts. Other posts on the page have 29.5K and 12.1K views, so this renting video (809K) is their big hit. (Correction: Instagram's 'm' means thousand, 'M' means million.) The bio sells a video-making tool, so the page is an automated showcase.
- **Format:** 64s, 1080×1920, **only 2 hard cuts**, voice almost continuous.

### What it does
1. **The hook is an A-vs-B question the viewer can answer:** "Which is cheaper: buying a €30,000 car, or renting?" Two cars appear side by side with price tags in the first 2s. The viewer makes a guess, then watches to see if they were right.
2. **Numbers never stop moving.** Money bars stack up beside each car while the year and month counters tick. There's something new to read every second, which carries the retention.
3. **"Almost the same" twist,** then a clear rule: at 4 years it's a near tie (€19,800 vs €21,600, then €24,800 with loan interest), at 7–10 years buying wins, and **"it depends on how long you keep the car."** That makes it save-worthy and gives people something to argue about.
4. **Personal money decision:** people send it to their partner or a friend who's deciding. That's why sends are higher than likes.
5. **Captions:** a short phrase at the top with one highlighted word (not our big centre word).
6. **Style:** a dark neon stage, one subject per scene, simple objects, money stacks, small number chips.
7. **CTA:** "¿Tú qué tienes? Te leo en comentarios" ("Which do you have? I'll read the comments"), with a big comment-bubble icon.
8. **Page brand:** the thumbnails show the **same cartoon face (mascot)** plus a topic icon and 2 words ("¿Mañana o noche?" got 29.5K). Its top topics are everyday body and habit questions.

### Patterns now seen in 2 references (one more and they become rules)
- **A recurring character or mascot** as the face of the page.
- **Very few hard cuts:** continuous scenes and camera moves.
- **Continuous voice** with no dead air.
- **Small labels and number chips,** no big flashy effects.
- **Personal or everyday relevance:** "your roof", "your car money".
- **A comment CTA that asks about the viewer's own life.**

### New ideas to test
- [ ] **An A-vs-B decision hook** ("X or Y? Which is better?"), answered with counters racing side by side.
- [ ] **Something on screen ticking every second** (counters, bars, timers).
- [ ] **An "it depends" rule at the end** that's worth saving.
- [ ] **Cover:** Hoot's face, a topic icon and a 2-word question.

## Ref #3: @datos.zip, "Un jet privado cayó 8 km en poco más de un minuto" (Spanish, same Learjet story as ours)
- **Numbers:** ~1M views **in 7 hours**, 38.2K likes (3.8%), 621 comments, 714 reposts, 10.3K sends (1%), 4.9K saves. The page has 7.1K followers and 20 posts. Its pinned hit, **"Vape or cigarette?"**, has 5.8M views (another A-vs-B).
- **Format:** 62.7s, 8 hard cuts, voice almost continuous.

### Compared with our Learjet video (same story)
| | Theirs | Ours |
|---|---|---|
| Length | **62s** | 90s |
| Hook | "How can a plane fall 8 km in one minute?" + red altitude counter | same idea (counter + title) ✓ |
| Lift and ice | **hand out of a car window**: tilt it and the air lifts it; ice changes the shape and the air no longer lifts | technical wing cross-section with flow lines |
| Speed comparison | **Formula 1 car at 350 km/h** vs the jet | Everest + skydiver |
| Impact | top-down view: road, traffic, crater | distant smoke (more respectful) |
| Ending | **"Was it the ice? … only the black box knows"**: an open mystery, people speculate in the comments | NTSB checklist + memorial |
| Captions | short phrase at the **top**, one highlighted word | one big word in the centre |

### Lessons
1. **Everyday analogies beat diagrams.** "Hand out of the car window" explains lift in 2 seconds. Everyone has done it.
2. **Compare to things people feel:** a car or F1 speed rather than abstract units.
3. **End on an open question or mystery** (the black box). It drives comments: people argue, guess, and ask things like "why not build planes from black-box material?".
4. **60s beats 90s** for news stories.
5. **Fast-moving news gets views quickly:** 1M in 7h on a 7K-follower page. Being fast matters.

## Ref #4: @nuncateloexplicaron, "¿Llenar el depósito hace que te dure más la gasolina?" (Spanish)
- **Numbers:** 24.3K likes, 584 reposts, 6.5K sends, 4.5K saves. The page has 76.3K followers and 49 posts, and other posts with 89.9K / 40.7K / 35.8K views (lowercase 'm' = thousand). It's labelled **"AI-generated content"**, and that doesn't stop it going viral.
- **Bio:** "You see it every day. But do you know how it works? … Understand the world in 60 seconds."
- **Format:** 92s, **only 4 hard cuts**, the same dark neon "blueprint" look as ref #2 (probably the same tool).

### What it does
1. **Myth hook about something you do every week:** "Does filling the tank to the top make your fuel last longer? Some people swear by it." A fuel gauge fills on screen.
2. **A chain of twists, each one flipping the answer:**
   1. Physics agrees: a full tank is 43 kg vs 22 kg, and a heavier car burns more.
   2. "But do the maths": the difference is a tiny glass of fuel.
   3. The detour to the petrol station costs more than that.
   4. The gauge lies: a float plus the tank's shape means the first half lasts "forever" and the second half drops fast.
   5. The pump is cooled by the fuel, so running low damages it.
   6. "The cheap option ends up expensive."
3. **Verdict as an A-vs-B:** full tank vs €20, plus "Truth or myth?" in the caption.
4. **Comment proof:** "So do I fill it up or not?" The leftover ambiguity makes people argue.
5. **Gauges, counters and kg chips** on screen the whole time.
6. **Captions:** a short phrase at the top with a highlighted word (the 3rd reference doing this).

## Ref #5: @comoefeito3d, "Por que o pombo balança a cabeça quando anda?" (Brazilian Portuguese, low-poly 3D like ours!)
- **Numbers:** 1.8M views, **160K likes (8.9%)**, 813 comments, 3.9K reposts, **35.4K sends (2%)**, 10.4K saves.
- **The page's grid (lowercase 'm' = thousand):** pigeon 1.8M, **"this water isn't dirt" (toilet U-bend) 666K**, gearbox 41.1K, "AC dripping on your bed?" 30.9K, sink 26.9K, washing machine 13.7K. A robot mascot logo, labelled AI-generated.
- **Format:** 73s, 9 hard cuts, cute low-poly 3D.

### What it does
1. **It asks about a weird thing everyone has SEEN but never understood:** "Why does the pigeon move its head back and forth when it walks?" The answer is visible in the first frame.
2. **Visual proof device:** a "SLOW MOTION 5x" chip plus a **vertical reference line** shows the head actually stays still while the body moves. You see it with your own eyes.
3. **Myth first, then the real answer:** "it's for balance… no, it's because of its eyes." An eye close-up, then a top view showing the eyes on the sides of its head.
4. **Real experiment:** a pigeon on a treadmill stops bobbing.
5. **Analogy to you:** "you look out of a train window and your eyes stick to a tree."
6. **Danger beat:** a cat approaching. The bob lets the pigeon spot movement.
7. **Covers:** bold 2-line text with a question on a 3D render ("WHY DOES THE PIGEON DO THIS?", "WHY DOES THE SPOON SPARK IN THE MICROWAVE?"). The profile grid reads like a list of questions you want answered.

### Key insight
**Household topics are NOT boring when they're framed as a weird observation.** Their U-bend video, framed as "This water isn't dirt: without it your house would stink!", got **666K**, their 2nd best, while a plainer sink video got 26.9K. Our sink video's problem was the framing ("why is this pipe U-shaped?" is a technical question), not the topic. Better framings are "This water isn't dirt: without it your house would stink" or "Why does your sink gurgle?"

### What it does
1. **Contrarian statement hook about your own body:** "Eight hours in bed don't guarantee you rested." Covers follow the same pattern: a bold statement plus an orange twist ("Night mode doesn't protect your sleep.", "Your brain isn't a muscle. But it shrinks.").
2. **One object becomes a chart:** the bed, then 8 stacked discs (hours), then bars of the sleep cycles (~90 min, 4–6 cycles). Clean data visuals, little text.

- **Comment-keyword CTA** ("Comment KNEW IT"): almost zero effort for the viewer, so lots of comments.

## Ref #7: @historiasdojoe, "NOITE 1 — RODOVIA 9" (Brazilian Portuguese, interactive survival fiction)
- **Numbers:** 2.2M views, **150K likes (6.8%)**, 1.8K reposts, **17.5K sends**, **12.3K saves**.
- **The page:** **27.1K followers from only 13 posts.** Series episodes keep getting views (449K, 285K, 227K, 220K…), and other posts have 148K–87K. The bio sells a course on making these.
- **Format:** 104s, 9 hard cuts, simple 2D stickman, bold white/yellow/red captions.

### What it does
1. **Second-person POV:** "you" are alone on the night shift at a petrol station, and you must survive 7 days.
2. **Series structure on screen:** a "NIGHT 1/7" chip, a clock (03:11), and **rules** ("Rule 2: lock the door. Rule 3: never use the window at night").
3. **Escalating tension:** lights go out one by one, an emergency alert, a stranger claiming to be the sheriff, a note in the drawer ("don't let him in, he came for me"), the radio says "all units… not him".
4. **Decision CTA:** "Would you open the door?" Comments become votes and debate.
5. **Real "next part" CTA:** "Follow for NIGHT 2". Here it's an actual continuing story, which is **why the page converts viewers into followers** (27K followers from 13 posts).

### Key insight for our follower problem
Standalone explainers get views but few follows: datos.zip has 1M views and 7K followers; explicadorapido has 29M views and 20K followers. **A continuing series gives people a reason to follow.** We can borrow the mechanics without leaving our lane:
- **An explainer series with continuity and choices**, for example "Hoot: Survive 7 days on the Moon", "Stranded at sea, day 1/7", or "Your plane is in trouble, part 1/5". Each episode solves a real science problem (oxygen, water, cold, radiation) and ends with "What would you do? A or B" plus "Follow for day 2".
- An **episode counter** on screen ("DAY 1/7").
- Covers that look like a set (same template, episode number).

## Ref #8: @construindoassim, "Como se constrói a casa que quase não paga luz" (Brazilian Portuguese, 3D cross-section like ours)
- **Numbers:** **1.3M views**, 120K likes (9%), 3.3K reposts, **21.3K sends**, **23.1K saves** (more saves than sends: useful money content), and a poll with 3,367 answers.
- **The page:** **53.1K followers from 38 posts**, with a builder-avatar profile picture and little gnome characters inside the videos. Grid (lowercase 'm' = thousand): this house 1.3M, mountain road 584K, cruise ship 29.7K, rocket base 203K, airport 53.9K, metro 60.8K.
- **Format:** 54s, **only 1 hard cut**, warm low-poly 3D house cross-section (very close to our style), one big centre word for captions (like ours).

### What it does
1. **A fixed cover template = a "format series":** every cover reads "COMO SE CONSTRÓI UM/UMA ___" (how they build a…) with a 2-line yellow-box title. People follow because they know what the next video will be, without needing a story.
2. **Personal benefit hook:** "the house that almost doesn't pay for electricity", which is about saving money.
3. **A chain of upgrades, each with a number chip:** solar panels → battery bank (battery %) → inverter (panel wave turns into a socket wave) → shower 7,300 W → solar water heater (water 28 °C → 60 °C) → AC 1,400 W → "Canadian well" (the ground at 2 m is ~22 °C all year) → the grid gives a bill discount → the whole house.
4. **Choice CTA:** "Which of these would you put in YOUR house? Comment", plus an Instagram poll.
5. **Little characters** live in the house and react.

### Lessons
- **A fixed title format plus cover template** works as a series: "HOOT EXPLAINS: WHY ___?" or "HOW ___ REALLY WORKS".
- **"Which one would you pick?"** list CTAs make easy comments.
- Number chips (W, °C, %) on every beat.
- Our style (low-poly cross-sections, centre-word captions) is proven at 1.3M. Caption style is not the bottleneck; topic and hook are.

## Ref #9: @battysticky, "5 ways to unf*ck your life" (English, self-improvement)
- **Numbers:** 1M views, 45.4K likes (4.5%), 3K reposts, 15.3K sends, **26K saves (2.6%, the highest save rate so far)**. The page has 13K followers from only 9 posts; its other hit, "5 Laws for Life", has **2.8M**.
- **Format:** 58.8s, **zero hard cuts** (one continuous animation), a yellow stick-figure **mascot** (also the profile picture) on black, small phrase captions at the top.

### What it does
1. **Numbered list ("5 ways…") with progress dots at the top** (● ○ ○ ○ ○). Viewers can see how many are left and stay to the end.
2. **One visual metaphor per point:** a ball rolling (a problem), a circle around "you" (boundaries), an umbrella in the rain (accepting reality), climbing stairs (insecurity), a steering wheel (emotions driving).
3. **Every cover uses the same template:** "N THINGS + big yellow/white title + the mascot" ("3 Levels Get Ahead", "5 Tricks to Break Bad Habits", "Learn Anything in 20 Hours").
4. **CTA stack:**
   - "Which one resonated most? **Comment 1–5**"
   - "**Save this** for a tough day"
   - "**follow** for more"
   - a **poll: "Which topic should I cover next?"** (the audience picks the next video, which gives them a reason to follow)

### Lessons
- **Numbered list plus progress dots** means retention and saves.
- **"Comment 1–5"** is the lowest-effort comment possible.
- **"Which topic next?" poll** makes the audience co-own the page, so they follow.
- **Mascot plus one cover template** again (now 5 of 9 pages).
