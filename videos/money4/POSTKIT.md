## Ready replies
- **"Which bank pays 4%?"** We don't recommend banks. Compare rates from several insured banks or credit unions, and read the fine print (minimums, intro rates).
- **"Banks need that spread to run!"** Partly true: branches and staff cost money. But research (Drechsler, Savov & Schnabl) finds banks pay less where they face less competition.
