# Platform Ranking Signals (Reels / TikTok / Shorts, 2025–2026) and What They Mean for 30–60 s Scripts

Context: an animated 2D cartoon explainer page (owl narrator plus a young guy character) covering money, how things work and the body, for a US audience. Research date: Nov 2026. Confidence labels: **[Official]** means the platform or its executive said it, citing a primary post or a faithful report of one. **[Reported]** means reputable press quoting an official source. **[3rd-party]** means a vendor or blog claim, low-to-medium confidence. **[Older]** means pre-2025 and possibly outdated.

## Instagram Reels: top signals, reaching non-followers, Trial Reels, originality, length

### Takeaway
Since January 2025 Mosseri has named three top Reels signals, all measured as rates, not totals: **watch time**, **likes per reach** and **sends (DM shares) per reach**. Sends count slightly more for reaching non-followers and likes count slightly more with followers. Watch time is counted in absolute seconds, not % completed. Originality is enforced harder every year: watermarked or reposted content loses non-follower recommendations, and from April 2026 accounts that mostly post unoriginal content are not recommended to non-followers at all. Reels can be up to 3 minutes long. Instagram says longer Reels are not penalized per se, but the signal is still total seconds watched.

### Cited Findings
- **[Official, Jan 2025]** Mosseri named watch time, likes and sends as the most important ranking signals ([Mosseri Instagram post DFFyRp-pINJ, via Buffer](https://www.instagram.com/p/DFFyRp-pINJ/); [Buffer guide](https://buffer.com/resources/instagram-algorithms/)). Social Media Today reported the remarks on Jan 22, 2025 ([cited via PostInstantly](https://postinstantly.com/guides/instagram-algorithm)). Comments were not on his list ([AIPoweredDahlia summary](https://aipowereddahlia.com/blog/instagram-algorithm-2026-what-works)).
- **[Official]** Likes are "slightly more important" for connected content (followers) and sends are slightly more important for unconnected content (non-followers) ([Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/instagram-algorithms/); [CreatorFlow](https://creatorflow.so/blog/instagram-algorithm/)). The widely repeated "sends are weighted 3–5x likes" figure is **not** Mosseri's wording. Treat it as unverified ([Keywords Everywhere tracker](https://keywordseverywhere.com/news/instagram-algorithm-updates/); [CreatorLane](https://creatorlanehq.com/blog/instagram-algorithm-2026-mosseri-vs-measured)).
- **[Official]** On his podcast appearance (Colin and Samir), Mosseri said shares, especially DMs, count for more when Instagram decides whether to show a post to non-followers. He also said about half of Instagram videos are watched without sound ([Buffer, citing YouTube interview](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HDzhA_UFrnA)). The interview date is not given. **[Possibly 2024–2025]**
- **[Official]** Mosseri tells creators to judge posts by rates, not raw views: "Too often creators look at how many views a post got or how many likes a reel got" ([Mosseri reel, via Buffer](https://www.instagram.com/reels/DdrY25_gNvi/)). In Sept 2026 he again told creators to focus on engagement rate rather than total views or likes ([Social Media Today, Sept 24, 2026](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-chief-shares-algorithm-insights-and-posting-tips/831326/)).
- **[Official, Feb 2025]** Watch time is counted in seconds, not completion %. Mosseri said: "you do *not* need to worry about whether or not people finish watching your whole video… What matters more is how many seconds of video they actually watch." By that logic, 10 seconds watched on a 3-minute Reel counts like 10 seconds on a 15-second Reel ([OnlineMarketing.de, Feb 26, 2025](https://onlinemarketing.de/social-media-marketing/instagram-so-wichtig-sind-watched-seconds), citing [Mosseri reel DGfycasy8VV](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DGfycasy8VV/)). Mosseri also said "we look at likes more because there are a lot more likes" (same source, secondhand).
- **[Official, early 2025]** Instagram added the **View Rate** insight: the share of viewers who keep watching past 3 seconds, split into followers and non-followers. It also added **Views Over Time**, which compares a post's views with your other posts ([Instagram Creators post DFaeuUyxKqZ](https://www.instagram.com/p/DFaeuUyxKqZ/), via [OnlineMarketing.de](https://onlinemarketing.de/social-media-marketing/instagram-so-wichtig-sind-watched-seconds)). Views have been the main metric across formats since summer 2024 (same source).
- **[Official, Sept 2026]** Mosseri described four principles: (1) originality, meaning originals get priority over reposts; (2) recency, meaning newer posts get more exposure; (3) "breaking" content, meaning posts from small creators are shown to non-followers to gauge response; and (4) bigger AI models weighing more factors. He recommended Trial Reels for testing and said creators "post as much as they can" to improve their odds while managing burnout ([Social Media Today, Sept 24, 2026](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-chief-shares-algorithm-insights-and-posting-tips/831326/)).
- **[Official, Dec 2025]** "Your Algorithm" lets users view and edit their inferred interests on Reels. It launched in December 2025 and has since reached Explore and Feed ([Instagram announcement](https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/reels-algorithm-control)). Users can also tap "Not interested" ([SMT 2026](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-chief-shares-algorithm-insights-and-posting-tips/831326/)).
- **Trial Reels [Reported/3rd-party]:** These launched in Dec 2024 ([eMarketer](https://www.emarketer.com/content/instagram-launches-trial-reels-empower-creators-test-new-formats); [Business Standard, Dec 11, 2024](https://business-standard.com/technology/tech-news/instagram-introduces-trial-reels-feature-what-it-is-how-it-works-more-124121100429_1.html)). A Trial Reel is shown first only to non-followers, through the Reels feed and Explore. It does not appear on the profile grid or in followers' feeds. After a test window the creator can share it to everyone or leave it ([Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/instagram-trial-reels/); [Inro](https://www.inro.social/blog/what-is-a-reel-trial-and-how-does-it-work)). **Conflicting [3rd-party] details:** the test window is given as about 24 h, 72 h or "24–72 h" ([Storrito](https://storrito.com/resources/how-instagram-trial-reels-work-72-hours/); [Fliki](https://fliki.ai/blog/trial-reels-instagram)). One source says Trial Reels are available to public professional accounts with 1,000+ followers.
- **Originality, 2024 [Reported, Apr 30, 2024]:** Instagram changed ranking to give smaller creators more chances and to replace recommended reposts with the original. It added labels on reposts that link to the original creator and removed aggregator accounts from recommendations. Content must be original and **without a watermark** to be recommendation-eligible ([Tubefilter](https://www.tubefilter.com/2024/04/30/instagram-meta-threads-original-content-algorithm-bonus-program-monetization/amp/); [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/instagrams-algorithm-overhaul-will-reward-original-content-and-penalize-aggregators-130018977.html); [Gigazine](https://www.gigazine.net/gsc_news/en/20240502-instagram-updated-algorithm-prioritizes-original-content)). **[Older but still in force.]**
- **Watermarks [Official, repeated]:** Instagram has said repeatedly that Reels carrying logos from other apps (TikTok, CapCut watermarks and so on) are pushed down. Your own branding is fine ([Instagram reel DBd7KE6T7E1, via Buffer](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DBd7KE6T7E1/?hl=en)).
- **Originality, 2026 [Reported, Apr 2026]:** Mosseri announced that accounts posting mostly unoriginal or aggregated content will not be recommended to non-followers. Their reach to existing followers is not limited. The policy now covers photos and carousels as well as Reels. Watermarks, minor crops or basic reposts are unlikely to count as "original"; the bar is meaningful transformation such as commentary or remixing ([PetaPixel, Apr 30, 2026](https://petapixel.com/2026/04/30/new-instagram-policies-target-reposted-content/); [Buffer, citing Mosseri reel DXwUplrhe4j](https://www.instagram.com/reels/DXwUplrhe4j/); [Digital Camera World](https://www.digitalcameraworld.com/tech/social-media/two-years-later-instagram-is-finally-giving-photographers-the-same-protection-as-videographers-with-this-key-change)). **[3rd-party, conflicting]** thresholds such as "10+ unoriginal posts in 30 days" and "eligibility returns after a 30-day fix" are not confirmed by Instagram.
- **Length [Official]:** The Reels maximum rose from 90 seconds to 3 minutes. Instagram's blog said "90 seconds isn't always enough time to tell a story just the way you want" ([creators.instagram.com](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/the-latest-with-instagram), via [OnlineMarketing.de](https://onlinemarketing.de/social-media-marketing/instagram-so-wichtig-sind-watched-seconds)). The announcement was in January 2025 ([Lindsey Gamble](https://www.lindseygamble.com/blog/instagram-three-minute-reels)). **[Older, May 2024]** At a creator event, Instagram's content team said "Reels that are longer than 90 seconds can hurt distribution" ([Social Media Today, May 13, 2024](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-says-posting-longer-reels-can-hurt-your-performance/715867/)). This was before the 3-minute change and likely superseded by Mosseri's 2025 "seconds watched" framing. **[3rd-party]** Claims that Reels over 3 minutes stop reaching non-followers rest on vendor interpretations of Socialinsider data and are not an Instagram statement ([Inro](https://www.inro.social/blog/instagram-reels-length)).
- **Hashtags [Official]:** Mosseri said "hashtags don't help reach" ([Mosseri reel C7opYxFxicG](https://www.instagram.com/mosseri/reel/C7opYxFxicG/)). Instagram capped hashtags at 5 per post or Reel, announced Dec 2025 ([@creators on Threads](https://www.threads.com/@creators/post/DSalXGPCWM4/), via [Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/instagram-algorithms/)).
- **Data study [Buffer, 4.2M posts]:** Reels got 36% more reach than carousels, while carousels got about 12% more engagement than Reels ([Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/instagram-reach-engagement-analysis/)). Buffer also found that across 100,000+ users, consistent posters got nearly 5x more engagement per post ([Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/consistent-posting-study/)). This is a correlation.

### Inferences
- For a 30–60 s cartoon explainer, the Instagram goal is to **maximize seconds watched and sends per viewer**. A send-worthy payoff is the main lever for non-follower reach: "send this to the friend who…", a surprising money fact, a body myth busted. Script one quotable, sendable reveal and land it before the end, not only in the last frame.
- About half of viewers watch muted, so **burned-in captions and visual storytelling are mandatory**. The owl's dialogue must read on screen, and the joke or reveal should work silently.
- Because watch time is absolute seconds, a strong 60 s piece is not penalized against a 30 s one. Length should be set by how long you can hold attention, not by a target duration. Padding that triggers drop-off still lowers total seconds.
- Export a clean master with no TikTok or CapCut watermark for Instagram. Original animation qualifies easily as "original"; the risk is only in reused clips or watermarked exports.
- Use Trial Reels to A/B test the first 2–3 seconds (two hook variants on the same body) and to test new topic pillars (money vs. body vs. how-things-work) without confusing followers.

### Gaps
- No primary Instagram documentation found that states the exact Trial Reels test window or eligibility threshold in 2026. Vendor sources conflict.
- No official statement on how loops or rewatches count in Reels watch time.
- The exact date and wording of the Colin and Samir interview were not verified.

## TikTok: For You ranking, search/SEO, Creator Rewards (>1 min)

### Takeaway
TikTok's only official ranking explainer (2020, still the canonical newsroom page) names user interactions (likes, shares, comments, follows, content created, "Not interested", hides, reports), video information (captions, sounds, hashtags) and lower-weight device/account settings. **Finishing a longer video is the example of a "strong" signal.** Follower count is not a direct factor. Since March 2024 the Creator Rewards Program pays only for **original videos over 1 minute**, scored on originality, play duration (watch time plus finish rate), **search value** and engagement. That pushed monetizing creators toward 61 s+ formats and search-friendly topics.

### Cited Findings
- **[Official, Jun 18, 2020, older but still the canonical page]** Recommendations draw on user interactions (videos liked or shared, accounts followed, comments posted, content created), video information (captions, sounds, hashtags) and device/account settings (language, country, device type). The last group carries lower weight "since users don't actively express these as preferences." Negative feedback ("Not Interested," hiding a creator or sound, reports) shapes future recommendations ([TikTok Newsroom](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/en-us/how-tiktok-recommends-videos-for-you/)).
- **[Official, 2020]** "A strong indicator of interest, such as whether a user finishes watching a longer video from beginning to end, would receive greater weight than a weak indicator" ([TikTok Newsroom](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/en-us/how-tiktok-recommends-videos-for-you/)).
- **[Official, 2020]** Neither follower count nor a history of high-performing videos is a direct factor. The feed avoids showing two videos in a row with the same sound or creator, and it does not recommend duplicates or spam. It also intentionally mixes in content outside a user's interests ([TikTok Newsroom](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/en-us/how-tiktok-recommends-videos-for-you/)).
- **[Official, Mar 18, 2024]** The Creator Rewards Program "will continue to reward high-quality, original content that is over a minute long." It scores four things: originality ("quality content unique to the creator"); play duration ("accounts for both watch time and finish rate"); search value ("assigned to content based on popular search terms… Content that aligns with in-demand search topics increases its value"); and audience engagement (likes, comments, shares). TikTok points creators to **Creator Search Insights** to find in-demand search topics ([TikTok Newsroom](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/introducing-the-new-creator-rewards-program?lang=en)).
- **[3rd-party]** Qualified views reportedly must come from the For You feed, last at least 5 seconds and not be marked "Not interested." Videos reportedly need 1,000+ qualified views to earn. Account eligibility is reportedly 18+, 10K followers and 100K views in the last 30 days ([Metricool](https://metricool.com/tiktok-creator-fund/); [Nuelink](https://blog.nuelink.com/tiktok-creator-rewards-program/)). One creator's example showed 175K qualified views out of 500K total ([Shortimize](https://www.shortimize.com/blog/qualified-views-vs-total-views)). Creator-reported RPM is roughly $0.40–$1.00 per 1,000 qualified views. That figure is unofficial.
- **[3rd-party, needs verification]** Since Sept 13, 2025, TikTok's Community Guidelines reportedly make unoriginal or reused content with no added contribution ineligible for the For You feed. On Jun 3, 2025 TikTok reportedly launched "Manage Topics" (sliders for 10+ topic categories) ([Blck Alpaca summary](https://blckalpaca.at/en/knowledge-base/social-media/social-media-algorithms-distribution/tiktok-algorithm-for-you-page.md); [SocialPilot](https://www.socialpilot.co/?p=702295)). I did not fetch the primary guideline text.
- **Search/SEO [3rd-party, consistent across vendors]:** TikTok search is said to index caption text, on-screen text (OCR), spoken words (auto speech recognition) and hashtags. The advice is to say the keyword aloud and put it in on-screen text in the first 0–3 s ([Socialk.it](https://socialk.it/en/blog/tiktok-seo-guide); [CapCut](https://www.capcut.com/create/tiktok-caption-keywords-discoverability)). No official TikTok source was found confirming that OCR or speech is used for ranking, though TikTok's own "search value" metric (above) confirms that search-aligned topics are rewarded. Uplift figures such as "340%" or "8x search impressions" are unverified vendor claims.

### Inferences
- **Format split:** a 30–45 s cut maximizes finish rate, the strongest stated signal. A 61–75 s cut is required for Creator Rewards payouts. Consider scripting a modular structure (hook, core explanation, bonus fact or twist) so one script yields a ~40 s Reels/Shorts cut and a 61 s+ TikTok cut without padding.
- **Write titles and opening lines as search queries** in US phrasing: "How does credit card interest actually work?", "Why do we get hiccups?". Have the owl say the exact phrase in the first sentence and show it as on-screen text. Use Creator Search Insights to pick topics. Money, body and how-it-works topics are naturally search-heavy evergreen categories.
- "Not interested" and fast swipes are negative signals, so a hook that misleads viewers about the payoff is costly beyond lost watch time.

### Gaps
- No 2025–2026 official TikTok explainer of For You ranking was found. The 2020 newsroom page remains the canonical source, and its weights may have changed.
- No official confirmation that TikTok ranks on spoken words or OCR text. Current Creator Rewards eligibility thresholds were not verified on TikTok's help page.
- No official statement on rewatch or loop weighting.

## YouTube Shorts: viewed vs. swiped away, view counting since March 2025, satisfaction, 3-minute length

### Takeaway
Since **March 31, 2025**, a Shorts "view" counts every time a Short starts or replays, with no minimum watch time. The old stricter metric survives as **Engaged views**, which drives YPP monetization. The key Studio diagnostic is **"Viewed vs. swiped away"**: the % of feed impressions where people watched rather than swiped. Shorts can be up to **3 minutes** (since Oct 15, 2024). Shorts over 1 minute with an active Content ID claim are blocked. Specific statements from Shorts product lead Todd Sherman exist only secondhand.

### Cited Findings
- **[Official, via TeamYouTube Community post, Mar 26, 2025; effective Mar 31, 2025]** A Shorts view is counted when a Short begins to play or replay, with no minimum watch time. The previous count is now "engaged views" (in Analytics Advanced Mode), described as showing "how many viewers choose to continue watching your Shorts." YPP eligibility and earnings remain based on engaged views. The Shorts YPP threshold is unchanged at 1,000 subscribers plus 10M valid public Shorts views in 90 days ([PPC Land](https://ppc.land/youtube-changes-how-shorts-views-are-counted-from-march-31/), citing [support.google.com/youtube/thread/333869549](https://support.google.com/youtube/thread/333869549); also [Digital Music News](https://www.digitalmusicnews.com/2025/03/27/how-does-youtube-shorts-count-views-2025/), [MobileSyrup](https://mobilesyrup.com/2025/03/27/youtube-changes-how-it-counts-shorts-views/)). YouTube advised using engaged views when comparing against pre-March 31 content. PPC Land says the change affects creator-facing metrics, not recommendation algorithms.
- **[Reported, Oct 3, 2024; effective Oct 15, 2024]** The Shorts maximum rose from 60 s to 3 minutes. YouTube said it would "improve recommendations for longer Shorts" in the coming months ([9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2024/10/03/youtube-shorts-3-minutes/); [Tubefilter](https://www.tubefilter.com/2024/10/03/youtube-shorts-three-minutes-max-length-redesign-trends-templates/); [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/youtube-expands-3-minute-shorts-to-all-users/736967/)). In January 2025, all uploads of 3 minutes or less in the app began registering as Shorts (same SMT coverage).
- **[Official, via PPC Land]** Shorts longer than 1 minute with an active Content ID claim are blocked globally: "The video will not be playable or recommended on YouTube, and will not be eligible for monetization" ([PPC Land](https://ppc.land/youtube-changes-how-shorts-views-are-counted-from-march-31/)). This matters for licensed music or sound in 61 s+ cuts.
- **Viewed vs. swiped away [3rd-party definition, consistent]:** "the percentage of times viewers watched your Short rather than swiping away," found in Studio's Shorts analytics. Some glossaries say the underlying analytics term is "Stayed to watch" ([Bytecap, Jul 2026](https://www.bytecap.io/research/youtube-shorts-retention-benchmarks); [Creator Essentials](https://www.creatoressentials.com/glossary/viewed-vs-swiped-away/)). I could not retrieve the official Help page directly.
- **Todd Sherman / Creator Insider [3rd-party, secondhand]:** Blogs report that Sherman (Shorts product lead) said Shorts views come mostly from the feed rather than deliberate choice, so ranking leans on viewer behavior. A new Short is first "explored" with a small seed audience and pushed wider if it performs ([vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/blog/post/youtube-shorts-algorithm/); [Medium/Ershov](https://medium.com/@AlexanderErshov/youtube-shorts-getting-0-views-heres-exactly-why-and-how-to-fix-it-84202c3eb6b3)). I was unable to verify the exact wording against the Creator Insider video.
- **Satisfaction [3rd-party framing]:** Blogs describe Shorts ranking as driven by swipe-away rate, average percentage viewed and satisfaction signals (likes, surveys) ([Quso](https://quso.ai/blog/youtube-shorts-algorithm); [Alpha.one](https://www.alpha.one/blog/how-does-the-youtube-shorts-algorithm-work)). This is consistent with YouTube's long-standing satisfaction messaging, but no 2025–2026 Shorts-specific primary statement was retrieved.

### Inferences
- With views now counting every replay, **raw Shorts views are inflated and not diagnostic**. Track Engaged views, Viewed-vs-swiped-away and average % viewed instead.
- The viewed-vs-swiped metric is entirely a first-1–2-second test. The opening frame must be visually arresting **before** any audio lands: the owl mid-action, a bold question card, a surprising visual. No logo sting, no "Hey guys."
- Seamless loops (the last line flows into the first frame) plausibly raise average % viewed (>100% is possible) and replays. This is a common creator tactic, but no official confirmation of rewatch weighting was found.
- For 61 s+ versions, use only original or properly licensed audio to avoid the Content ID block.

### Gaps
- Official YouTube Help text for "Viewed vs. swiped away" and any official statement on how loops or rewatches affect Shorts ranking were not retrieved.
- No primary quote from Todd Sherman was verified. No evidence was found on whether YouTube's promised "improved recommendations for longer Shorts" happened.

## Cross-platform metrics to watch and benchmark numbers

### Takeaway
Each platform exposes an early-hold metric: Instagram **View Rate** (past 3 s), YouTube **Viewed vs. swiped away**, and TikTok retention/finish rate. Each also exposes a depth metric (IG average watch time; YT average % viewed and engaged views; TikTok play duration/finish rate) and a distribution metric (IG sends per reach and likes per reach; shares on TikTok and YT). **No platform publishes official "good" benchmarks.** Every numeric threshold circulating online is creator or vendor opinion.

### Cited Findings
- Instagram's own insights include View Rate (% watching past 3 s, follower vs. non-follower) and Views Over Time ([Instagram Creators post](https://www.instagram.com/p/DFaeuUyxKqZ/), via [OnlineMarketing.de](https://onlinemarketing.de/social-media-marketing/instagram-so-wichtig-sind-watched-seconds)). Likes per reach and sends per reach are calculated as likes (or shares) divided by accounts reached ([Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/instagram-algorithms/)).
- YouTube publishes no universal "good retention" threshold. The recommended practice is to compare within length cohorts (15–30 s, 30–60 s, >60 s) on your own channel ([Bytecap evidence synthesis, Jul 17, 2026, no sample](https://www.bytecap.io/research/youtube-shorts-retention-benchmarks); [Creator Essentials](https://www.creatoressentials.com/glossary/viewed-vs-swiped-away/)).
- **[3rd-party, low confidence]** Viewed-vs-swiped benchmarks conflict. One source says 70–90% viewed for top performers and <60% is weak; another says 70/30 is "viral territory" and 50/50 is average ([ReelRise](https://reelrise.app/guide/viewed-vs-swiped-away-the-only-youtube-shorts-metric-that-matters/); [Debutify](https://debutify.com/blog/short-video-algorithm)). None gives a sample or methodology.
- **[3rd-party, unverified]** Average retention is reportedly about 78% on TikTok, about 73% on Shorts and about 65% on Reels, attributed to "Socialinsider 2025 data" by a Virvid blog. The original was not found ([Virvid](https://virvid.ai/blog/ai-shorts-increase-retention-watch-time)). Windsor.ai says there is no reliable external benchmark for Reels watch time ([Windsor.ai](https://windsor.ai/instagram-reels-retention-chatgpt/)).
- **Data study sample:** Socialinsider analyzed 25,503,389 Reels from brands with 1K–1M followers (Jan–Dec 2025) on how long Reels take to accumulate views ([Socialinsider](https://www.socialinsider.io/blog/how-long-does-it-take-for-reels-to-get-views/)). The specific findings were not extracted.
- **Data study:** Buffer, 4.2M Instagram posts, Reels reach vs. carousels (see above) ([Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/instagram-reach-engagement-analysis/)).

### Inferences
- Suggested per-video dashboard for this page, all compared against the channel's own rolling median rather than external benchmarks:
  1. Early hold: IG View Rate / YT Viewed-vs-swiped / TikTok 2–3 s retention.
  2. Depth: IG average watch time in seconds / YT average % viewed and engaged views / TikTok average watch time and finish rate.
  3. Spread: IG sends per reach and likes per reach / TikTok shares and new follows / YT subscribers gained per 1K engaged views.
  4. Retention-graph cliffs, to locate exactly which script line loses people.
- Treat any external "% viewed" target as a rough heuristic at best. Iterate hooks with Trial Reels and A/B variants.

### Gaps
- No credible, methodology-disclosed 2025–2026 cross-platform retention or hold-rate benchmark study was found. Later, Hootsuite and Dash Hudson Reels/Shorts retention studies were not retrieved.

## Common reach-killing mistakes

### Takeaway
The best-documented killers are **unoriginal or watermarked content** (IG and TikTok explicitly), **weak first seconds** (measured directly by IG View Rate and YT Viewed-vs-swiped), and **misleading hooks or bait that trigger "Not interested" and fast swipes**. Others: **relying on hashtags** (Mosseri: they don't help reach; IG caps them at 5), **licensed music in >1 min Shorts** (Content ID block), and **posting duplicate content** (TikTok does not recommend duplicates). Posting cadence is a correlation, not a ranking rule. No primary source was found for "deleting and reposting kills reach."

### Cited Findings
- **Watermarks / cross-posting TikTok exports to IG:** IG pushes down Reels with other apps' logos and requires recommendation-eligible content to have no watermark ([Instagram reel via Buffer](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DBd7KE6T7E1/?hl=en); [Engadget 2024](https://www.engadget.com/instagrams-algorithm-overhaul-will-reward-original-content-and-penalize-aggregators-130018977.html)).
- **Reposting or aggregating:** Instagram replaces reposts with originals in recommendations (2024). From April 2026, accounts that mostly post unoriginal content are not recommended to non-followers ([PetaPixel](https://petapixel.com/2026/04/30/new-instagram-policies-target-reposted-content/); [Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/instagram-algorithms/)). TikTok does not recommend duplicate content or spam ([TikTok Newsroom 2020](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/en-us/how-tiktok-recommends-videos-for-you/)), and Creator Rewards scores originality ([TikTok 2024](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/introducing-the-new-creator-rewards-program?lang=en)).
- **Slow intros:** IG measures View Rate past 3 s ([OnlineMarketing.de](https://onlinemarketing.de/social-media-marketing/instagram-so-wichtig-sind-watched-seconds)). YT measures viewed vs. swiped away ([Creator Essentials](https://www.creatoressentials.com/glossary/viewed-vs-swiped-away/)). Both are first-seconds tests.
- **Misleading hooks / clickbait:** "Not Interested," hides and reports are explicit negative signals on TikTok ([TikTok Newsroom](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/en-us/how-tiktok-recommends-videos-for-you/)). IG offers "Not interested" and Your Algorithm interest editing ([SMT 2026](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-chief-shares-algorithm-insights-and-posting-tips/831326/)). TikTok qualified views exclude viewers who mark "Not interested" ([Metricool, 3rd-party](https://metricool.com/tiktok-creator-fund/)).
- **Hashtag stuffing:** "hashtags don't help reach" (Mosseri). IG limit is 5 per post ([Mosseri](https://www.instagram.com/mosseri/reel/C7opYxFxicG/); [@creators Threads](https://www.threads.com/@creators/post/DSalXGPCWM4/)).
- **Chasing raw views:** Mosseri says to judge by rates ([SMT 2026](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-chief-shares-algorithm-insights-and-posting-tips/831326/)). YT views now count every replay ([PPC Land](https://ppc.land/youtube-changes-how-shorts-views-are-counted-from-march-31/)).
- **Posting cadence:** Mosseri says creators post as much as they can to improve their odds of a hit while avoiding burnout. That is a volume/odds framing, not a frequency penalty or reward ([SMT 2026](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-chief-shares-algorithm-insights-and-posting-tips/831326/)). Buffer's 100K-user study finds consistent posters get about 5x engagement per post, a correlation only ([Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/consistent-posting-study/)).
- **Licensed audio in long Shorts:** Shorts >1 min with an active Content ID claim are blocked from playback and recommendation ([PPC Land](https://ppc.land/youtube-changes-how-shorts-views-are-counted-from-march-31/)).
- **Comment-bait:** Mosseri's top-signal list omits comments ([AIPoweredDahlia](https://aipowereddahlia.com/blog/instagram-algorithm-2026-what-works)), so "comment X below" prompts optimize a non-headline signal on IG. TikTok and YouTube do count comments as engagement ([TikTok 2024](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/introducing-the-new-creator-rewards-program?lang=en)).

### Inferences
- **Script checklist:** cold-open on the payoff question within the first ~1 s, with on-screen text, no logo intro, no "in this video." Keep a high information or visual-change rate. Put one sendable "wow" line around the midpoint. End on a loopable final beat rather than a long outro or "follow for more" card. Captions should be burned in.
- **Production pipeline:** keep a watermark-free master per platform and native uploads only. Use original score or SFX for 61 s+ TikTok and Shorts versions. Use at most 3–5 relevant hashtags plus keyword-rich captions.
- Use a sendable CTA ("send this to someone who still thinks…") rather than engagement bait. It aligns with IG's sends-per-reach signal without misleading viewers.

### Gaps
- No primary platform statement was found on whether deleting and re-uploading the same video hurts reach. It is widely claimed but unverified here. TikTok's duplicate-content rule suggests re-uploads may be deprioritized as duplicates; that is an inference only.
- No official evidence was found on optimal posting frequency per platform, or on whether posting time affects ranking beyond IG's stated "recency" principle.
