# A Vida do Tavinho: 2 series (approved by the owner on 5 Nov 2026)

**Format (owner, 5 Nov 2026):**
- Each story is a series of days. Only the starting problem is fixed. **Every action Tavinho takes is the audience's choice in the comments.**
- Each video is one event and ends on one vote. The next video opens with "Vocês escolheram X…", shows what that caused, then poses the next choice.
- The days can jump ("dia 3", "dia 8"), so a series is about 6–8 videos.
- The owner reads the comments (or a Stories poll) and tells us the winning option. We can prepare both branches in advance.
- **Narration only:** one ElevenLabs voice, "Will - Deep, Smooth and Affectionate" (`CstacWqMhJQlnfLPxRG4`). No character speaks; their lines show only as text bubbles.
- **Humour for adults, not kids:** cringe, irony, office politics, a passive-aggressive mother-in-law. No slapstick for children.
- **Each series counts time differently:**
  - "O áudio": 12 days. A countdown chip in the corner; the narration says "três dias depois", "na véspera".
  - "A cachorra": 15 days. "Dia três", "dia oito", counting up.
- Rules check (SCRIPT_RULES.md):
  - the hook says who and what
  - the "você decide" bridge comes at about 5 s
  - turns are "mas" or "então"
  - the follow ask is mid-video
  - each video has a twist at about 70%
  - the vote has a real cost and a downside on each side
  - the video ends on the question
  - numbers are written as words

The scripts' JSON is in `videos/tavaudio/script.json` and `videos/tavcachorra/script.json`.
