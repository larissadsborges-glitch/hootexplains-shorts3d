## Ready replies
- **"7% is unrealistic."** The US market's 1928–2025 average was about 10% a year before inflation, about 6.8% after (NYU Stern + BLS). We rounded our results to stay true at 6.7%. Nothing is guaranteed, though.
- **"So you're saying don't buy coffee?"** The opposite. The amount was small. Starting early is what did the work.
- **"What should I invest in?"** We don't give picks. Investor.gov (the SEC's site) has free basics on fees and diversification.
