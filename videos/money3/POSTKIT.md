## Ready replies
- **"Some stuff got cheaper!"** True, some did. CPI is the average across a basket of what people buy, so some prices rose more than average and some less.
- **"Why would anyone want inflation?"** The Fed says low, steady inflation helps people make sound decisions about saving, borrowing and investing. Its target is 2%, measured by the PCE price index.
