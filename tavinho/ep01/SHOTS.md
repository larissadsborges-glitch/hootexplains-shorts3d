# EP1 "A mentira": shot plan (per caption phrase)

Each row is one caption phrase (2–3 words, about 1 s) and one shot.

**Shot types:**
- **WIDE:** establishing shot of the place
- **TWO:** two-shot
- **OTS:** over the shoulder, with a blurred foreground
- **FACE:** a giant face close-up, every 3–5 s
- **INS:** an insert (a detail, a finger on the phone)
- **FRZ:** a freeze-frame (white flash and a label)

**Moves:**
- **push:** 1.0 → 1.08 over the shot
- **drift:** ±30 px
- **roll:** 3–5° on drama
- **shake:** 7 frames on impacts

Expressions change on the phrase. The narrator is never lip-synced.
