# Explainland: video ideas backlog

Every idea meets the retention checklist:
- **Hook:** the first 2s show the title's promise as the eye-catching image (passes the "sound off" test).
- **Story:** a failure → fix chain, with a "but…" every 15–20s.
- **Surprise:** one mid-video analogy, number or sense of scale.
- **Payoff:** at the end, then a CTA question.
- **Realism:** every step real. Verify facts with a quick search before scripting.

## 1. What happens if an elevator cable snaps?
- **Hook:** a cut-away shaft with people inside; the cable snaps and the car drops.
- **Chain:**
  1. Freeze, then rewind to why it almost never happens.
  2. 4–8 cables, each able to hold the car alone.
  3. A counterweight balances it.
  4. *Re-hook:* "what if all fail?"
  5. A speed governor detects the fall.
  6. Safety brakes wedge into the rails.
  7. Buffers at the bottom of the pit.
- **Surprise:** Otis cut his own rope in front of a crowd in 1854.
- **Payoff:** the brakes clamp and the car stops a metre down.
- **CTA:** "Still taking the stairs?"

## 2. How does a crane get on top of a skyscraper?
- **Hook:** a crane on a 300 m tower, tilting down the drop.
- **Chain:**
  1. A mobile crane assembles a small tower crane.
  2. *Re-hook:* the building outgrows it.
  3. A climbing frame jacks it up and inserts mast sections; ties fix it to the building.
  4. *Re-hook:* "how does it get DOWN?" Successively smaller cranes dismantle each other; the last one goes down in the lift.
- **CTA:** "Did you know this?"

## 3. Why don't skyscrapers snap in the wind?
- **Hook:** a tower swaying in a storm with a giant golden ball swinging inside.
- **Chain:**
  1. Wind makes the top sway metres.
  2. Deep piles and a concrete core keep it standing.
  3. *Re-hook:* "people get seasick."
  4. A tuned mass damper (Taipei 101: ~660 t) swings against the sway.
- **Surprise:** a person on a bus leaning into the turn; the ball weighs as much as ~500 cars.
- **CTA:** "Would you live on the top floor?"

## 4. How do buildings survive earthquakes?
- **Hook:** two identical buildings shake; one falls, one glides.
- **Chain:**
  1. A stiff building cracks.
  2. Steel bracing helps.
  3. *Re-hook:* "what if it didn't touch the ground?"
  4. Base isolators (lead-rubber bearings).
  5. Dampers absorb the rest.
- **Surprise:** a skateboard on a shaking floor.
- **CTA:** "Would your house survive?"

## 5. How do ships climb over mountains? (Panama Canal)
- **Hook:** a cargo ship rising up a staircase of water: "about to climb 26 metres."
- **Chain:**
  1. The land is too high for a sea-level canal.
  2. Locks: a chamber, gates close, valves flood it and the ship rises.
  3. *Re-hook:* "where does the water come from?" Gravity from Gatun Lake, no pumps.
  4. Down the other side to the Pacific.
- **Surprise:** ~200 million litres of fresh water per passage (verify).
- **CTA:** "Pacific or Atlantic?"

## 6. How does a dam hold back a whole lake?
- **Hook:** a cross-section with a lake on one side and a tiny town below.
- **Chain:**
  1. Divert the river through tunnels to build dry.
  2. Pressure grows with depth (triangle of arrows), so the wall is thicker at the base (Hoover ~200 m vs ~14 m at the crest).
  3. *Re-hook:* overflow → spillway.
  4. Turbines make electricity.
- **Surprise:** the arch pushes the force into the canyon walls (an egg pushed end-on).
- **CTA:** "Would you live below it?"

## 7. Why doesn't your bathroom smell like a sewer?
- **Hook:** a house cut-away with a green stink cloud rising from the sink.
- **Chain:**
  1. A straight pipe lets the gas rise.
  2. P-trap water seal.
  3. *Re-hook:* "flushing siphons it out!"
  4. A vent pipe through the roof.
- **Surprise:** that pipe on every roof; viewers check their own house.
- **CTA:** "Go look at your roof."

## 8. How do they build bridges in the middle of the sea?
- **Hook:** a tower rising out of open ocean in waves: "how do you pour concrete underwater?"
- **Chain:**
  1. A cofferdam is driven down and pumped dry.
  2. Piles go down to rock, then a pier.
  3. *Re-hook:* "connect towers with no ground between?"
  4. Balanced cantilever segments on both sides.
  5. The halves meet in the middle with only cm of error.
- **CTA:** "Would you drive it?"

## 9. Why do roads keep getting potholes?
- **Hook:** a tyre slams into a pothole with a splash.
- **Chain:**
  1. Water gets into cracks.
  2. It freezes and expands (~9%).
  3. It melts and leaves a hollow; traffic breaks it.
  4. *Re-hook:* "how do good roads last decades?"
  5. Layers + camber + drains.
- **CTA:** "Worst road near you?"

## 10. Why are water towers up on legs?
- **Hook:** a giant ball on stilts above a town.
- **Chain:**
  1. Morning demand outruns the pumps.
  2. The tower stores water high up (10 m ≈ 1 bar).
  3. Pumps refill it at night.
  4. *Re-hook:* the water must be cleaned first (treatment steps).
- **CTA:** "Ever noticed one?"

**Recommended first:** #1 (strongest hook), #5 (most striking, quick to build), #7 (most comments and shares).
